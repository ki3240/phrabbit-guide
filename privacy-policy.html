English

Phrabbit ("the app") is an audio loop-practice app for language learning. Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what data the app does and does not handle.

1. No account, no data collection by the developer

The app does not require any account or login, and the developer does not collect, store, or transmit your personal information to its own servers. The free version shows ads through Google AdMob — see section 6.

2. Your audio stays on your device

Audio files you import and any A/B loops, bookmarks, transcripts, or wordbook entries you create are stored locally on your device. The developer does not receive or have access to them.

3. Speech-to-Text

Automatic speech-to-text is performed using Apple's built-in Speech framework. Depending on your device and iOS version, recognition may run on-device or be processed by Apple. This is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. The developer never collects or stores your audio or the resulting text.

4. Podcasts

If you use the podcast feature, the app connects directly to third-party podcast providers to fetch feeds and episodes. Those requests are subject to the respective providers' privacy policies. The developer does not operate these services and does not collect data from them.

5. Purchases

The optional one-time premium purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your payment details.

6. Advertising (free version)

After the 72-hour Premium pass ends, the free version shows banner ads provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC). During the pass and for Premium purchasers, no ads are shown and the ad SDK is not started.

  • Ads are non-personalized and limited to content suitable for a general audience. The app does not ask for permission to track you and does not use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may receive information such as your IP address (approximate location), device and app information, an app-specific device identifier, and your interactions with ads. This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
  • The app never sends your audio, transcripts, wordbook entries, file names, or any other content you create to Google.
  • Where the law requires consent, the app asks for your choice through Google's consent message, and you can change it at any time in Settings > Ads.
  • You can report an inappropriate ad in Settings > Ads. This opens an email in your mail app containing the ad's identifiers, the app version and the iOS version; nothing is sent unless you send it.

7. Data deletion

Because the developer collects no data, deleting the app removes all locally stored content from your device.

8. Children

The app is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy: phrabbit.support@gmail.com

  • 본 앱은 사용자의 오디오, 자막(텍스트), 단어장, 파일 이름 등 사용자가 만든 어떤 콘텐츠도 Google에 보내지 않습니다.
  • 법률상 동의가 필요한 지역에서는 Google의 동의 메시지로 선택을 요청하며, 설정 > 광고에서 언제든지 변경할 수 있습니다.
  • 부적절한 광고는 설정 > 광고에서 신고할 수 있습니다. 광고 식별자, 앱 버전, iOS 버전이 담긴 이메일이 메일 앱에 작성되며, 사용자가 직접 보내기 전에는 아무것도 전송되지 않습니다.

7. 데이터 삭제

개발자가 어떤 데이터도 수집하지 않으므로, 앱을 삭제하면 기기에 저장된 모든 콘텐츠가 함께 삭제됩니다.
